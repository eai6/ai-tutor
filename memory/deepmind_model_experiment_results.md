### Three findings (full narrative in `memory/deepmind_findings_writeup.md`)

1. **Tool-use compliance is bimodal at this prompt shape.** Anthropic 48–81%; Gemini and OpenAI 0%. Anthropic-flavored tool spec doesn't translate to Gemini's `function_calling_config` or OpenAI's `tool_choice='required'`. **Prompt-adaptation gap, not model-capability gap.**
2. **Session completion diverges sharply.** Anthropic 12/12 → exit_ticket. Gemini 6/12. OpenAI 5/12. Without tools, no advance — models loop until the deadlock detector kicks them.
3. **Cycle-1 regen-clean rate is the truest instruction-following signal.** Opus 60%, Haiku 50%, Sonnet 4%, every non-Anthropic **0%** across 128 combined rewrite attempts.

### Notable per-cell highlights

- **Gemini 3.1 Pro completed math-struggler (L638) in 27 prose-only turns**, ~4× the Anthropic pace, in 889s. Demonstrates Gemini 3 Pro CAN navigate the lesson without tools — just slowly and at high `no_question` flag rate.
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash and GPT-5 deadlocked on all 4 cells each** within 90s. They never advanced past the warmup question.
- **Haiku 4.5 holds 50% cycle-1 clean rate** — within 20% of Opus 4.7. Suggests pilot tutoring could run on Haiku + judge ensemble at ~⅓ the per-token cost.

### Accuracy note (validator-flag proxy)

The "100% of turns flagged" on Anthropic models is dominated by two noisy validators (`numeric_claim_unverified`, `figure_ref_without_signal`) — ungrounded ≠ wrong. The signature flag profiles are more informative:

| model | distinctive flag (per 10 turns) |
|---|---|
| Anthropic (any) | `figure_ref_without_signal` 4.4–4.7 + `repeated_question` 2.1–3.8 |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro | **`no_question` 5.9** (highest by far — direct symptom of 0% tool-use) |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash | **`authoring_violation` 4.1** (authored prose MCQs that should be tool calls) |
| OpenAI (any) | low overall flag rate, but only because sessions died short |

True per-turn accuracy requires the post-hoc LLM meta-judge in
`memory/deepmind_infrastructure_session.md` #5a (~$5–10, 1.5 days).
