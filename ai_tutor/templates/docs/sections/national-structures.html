THE MULTI-TENANCY INVARIANT

Every query that touches user data is scoped to an institution, with platform-wide content included through an explicit "this school or everyone" filter. A national instance runs every school as an institution and shares one national curriculum at the platform-wide tier. Any new query that forgets the scope is a cross-school data leak, which is why it is enforced as a codebase-wide rule with tests rather than left to review.
