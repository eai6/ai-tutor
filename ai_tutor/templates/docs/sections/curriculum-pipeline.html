WHY THE TWO PACK FORMATS REFUSE EACH OTHER

A curriculum pack seeds a foreign deployment and deliberately carries no roster. A device provisioning pack carries a roster, because a school laptop must bind local logins to server accounts, and it travels on a USB stick inside one school. The importers reject each other's files outright rather than importing the safe subset — because otherwise the mistake is made by whoever copies the wrong file, long after anyone is watching, and it would hand another country's children's names to a third party.

The generation pipeline

  • Upload the syllabus for a subject, choosing the page range for each grade. Re-uploading edits by additive merge rather than wiping what reviewers have already approved.
  • Parse into the course, unit and lesson hierarchy.
  • Generate eight to twelve teaching steps per lesson, in the course language, with worked examples and vocabulary.
  • Generate figures — educational diagrams, with prompt engineering aimed at not inventing data that is not in the lesson.
  • Generate exit tickets — roughly thirty-five questions per lesson, of which ten are presented per attempt.
  • Extract skills and infer lesson prerequisites, building the graph that gates a student from starting a lesson they are not ready for.
  • Index curriculum text, exam questions and teaching materials into the vector knowledge base for retrieval during tutoring. Embeddings are computed locally, so this step needs no external service.
  • Review and publish in the dashboard. Nothing reaches students unreviewed unless you choose to let it.

Practical notes

  • Figures are the gap in a bare deployment. Roughly three quarters of lesson steps reference a figure. The publicly distributed image ships lessons but not media, so those steps read with a hole until media is generated or uploaded. Plan figure generation as part of Phase 2, not as a finishing touch.
  • Generation failures leave lessons in a generating state and need a manual reset to pending — worth a line in the operations runbook.
  • Content rights travel with the content. Curriculum derived from a national syllabus may carry its own terms; check before redistributing a pack to another country.
  • Review throughput is the schedule. Measure it in the first fortnight — lessons reviewed per expert per day — and re-plan Phase 2 against the real number.
