Three harnesses ship with the platform, at three units of analysis. Use all three; they answer different questions.

HarnessUnitAnswers
Runtime judgeOne tutor reply"Should this reply be shown, and should this session be flagged?"
Turn-level workbenchOne tutor turn from a real production session"Which specific failure categories does the tutor exhibit on our content?" Human and model annotators score the same turns.
Session-level rubricA complete session"Is this good tutoring?" Scored on the eight pedagogical dimensions of the standard research framework, three-way.

A fourth harness runs offline model sweeps — replaying scenarios across candidate models and reporting quality, latency, token usage and cost per run. This is the tool for the sovereignty decision: it produces a like-for-like comparison of a self-hosted model against a cloud model on your own curriculum, which is the only comparison worth making.

DO NOT GATE ON JUDGES ALONE

Measured on 215 hand-graded sessions from the live pilot: 50 judge-versus-human disagreements, every one of them a session the judge passed and a human failed, and none in the other direction. Judges are lenient. Use them to triage what humans read, and let humans decide whether a locale is ready.
