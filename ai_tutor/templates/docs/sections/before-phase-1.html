If a country can answer these, implementation can start. If it cannot, implementation will start anyway and stall on the unanswered one.

  • Where may model calls run? In the vendor's cloud, in a specific jurisdiction, or only inside the country? Who signs that off?
  • Who operates the deployment, and on whose cloud account or hardware?
  • Which subjects and which grades are in scope for the first cohort?
  • Do the syllabus documents exist in a usable form, and may they be used to derive teaching content?
  • Who reviews generated content, how many hours per week do they have, and who signs a lesson off as fit to teach?
  • Who settles the language register, and is that person a practising educator?
  • What are the grade codes and labels exactly as students should see them?
  • What is the device and connectivity reality in the pilot schools — individual devices, shared devices, or no reliable connection?
  • Who reads flagged sessions, and what happens when one indicates a student is at risk?
  • What is the budget ceiling for model usage, and who is watching it weekly?
