Roles that need a name against them

RoleResponsibility
Curriculum authorityApproves generated content as fit to teach. Owns the review backlog and the standard applied to it.
Technical ownerRuns the deployment: infrastructure, upgrades, backups, secrets, monitoring. One named person or team, in country.
Data controller / protection officerOwns the impact assessment, the consent and retention posture, and subject access requests.
Safeguarding leadReviews flagged sessions and owns the escalation path when a student message indicates risk.
Teacher championsTwo or three per pilot school. The difference between a platform that is used and one that is installed.

What the country supplies

  • Syllabus documents in PDF or DOCX, per subject and grade, and confirmation that they may be used to derive teaching content.
  • Subject expert review time — the largest single commitment, and the one most often under-budgeted.
  • A native-speaking educator to settle language register and review generated content.
  • The school and grade register as it should appear to students.
  • A hosting decision and a data protection assessment.
  • Teacher time for training and for weekly review during the pilot.
  • A device and connectivity baseline per pilot school, which determines whether that school is online, offline or shared-device.

What the platform supplies

  • The application, its deployment tooling for four topologies, and its documentation — a self-hosting manual, technical reference, teacher guide and student guide.
  • The content pipeline and the evaluation harnesses.
  • Locale onboarding for a new language and register.
  • A bundled sample curriculum so a deployment is testable on day one, before any national content exists.
