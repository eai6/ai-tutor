Two sequencing rules earn their keep:

  • Do not start Phase 2 before Phase 1 is genuinely finished. Content generated against an unsettled locale or grade structure is content generated twice.
  • Run Phase 2 per subject, not per country. One subject through to a validated pilot teaches more than four subjects half-reviewed, and it gives teachers something real to react to while the rest is built.
