# Student Guide — AI Tutor Platform

How to use the platform from a student's perspective.

## Signing in

1. Go to the home page and click Sign In.
2. Use the username and password your teacher gave you.
3. If you forgot your password, click Forgot password — a reset
   email arrives in a minute (assuming your email is verified).

## Picking a subject + lesson

1. Your home page shows the subjects available to you (e.g.
   Mathematics S3, Geography S3) plus a yellow "This week's
   lessons" panel with what your teacher assigned for the week.
2. Click on a subject card to open its lesson list.
3. Tap any lesson to start.
4. Lessons you've already mastered show a green check; the
   platform suggests a recommended next one.

## Choosing how long a lesson takes

When you open a lesson, you'll see a small purple banner at the
top: "⏱️ How long do you have today?" with quick choices.

- **15 min** — quick run with 3 essential steps (engage + teach +
  check)
- **20 min** — adds a practice step
- **30 min** — the standard depth: warm-up, teach, worked example,
  two practices, quiz
- **Use the lesson default** — your teacher's setting (usually 20
  min)

The picker waits until you click — no time pressure. You answer
the same teaching objective either way; shorter sessions just
skip the enrichment steps.

If you don't see the banner, your teacher has locked the duration
for everyone. Sessions use the teacher's default in that case.

## During a lesson

The tutor is conversational — type your answer the way you'd write
it on paper. The tutor will guide you when you're stuck.

### If a problem feels too hard or too easy

Tap the **Too hard** or **Too easy** button in the chat header.
The tutor adjusts.

### If the tutor doesn't understand your answer

Try rephrasing in shorter sentences, or use numbers / symbols
(e.g. `x = 4`). Math lessons have a small purple toolbar above
the chat input with quick-insert buttons for °, π, √, ², ±, etc.
The grader is forgiving — typing `38` and `38°` count as the
same answer.

### Audio

Tap the speaker icon on a tutor message to hear it read aloud.

## Group sessions (sharing a device)

If your school uses shared devices, your teacher will have
created a group for you. Before starting a lesson:

1. Open the lesson — at the top you'll see your name and a
   "+ Add groupmate" button.
2. Tap it. A list of students in your group appears.
3. Tap the name of the student sitting next to you. They join
   immediately, no password needed.

If you don't see the "+ Add groupmate" button, your school is in
"individual" mode (everyone takes lessons solo) or your teacher
hasn't put you in a group yet. Tell your teacher.

## Exit tickets

At the end of every lesson there's a short exit ticket — 10
questions drawn from a 35-question bank, mixed across five
formats:
- Multiple choice (4 options, pick a letter)
- Fill in the blank
- True / False
- Short answer (1–2 sentences in your own words)
- Data interpretation (read a chart or diagram and answer)

Pass and you've mastered the lesson. The pass threshold is shown
at the top of the ticket.

If you don't pass, the tutor walks you through what you missed
and you can retry. Mastery is permanent — once you've earned it,
it stays on your record even if your teacher reworks the lesson
later.

You can review your finished exit tickets on the lesson page.

## Course exam (baseline + retake)

Each course has a summative exam — a 30-question check across the
whole course. You take it twice (or more):

- **Baseline** — before you start any lessons. Don't worry about
  scoring high; we use it to figure out which lessons to focus on.
- **Final** — after you've worked through the course. The Δ between
  baseline and final tracks your growth.
- **Retakes** — once you have any attempt, a "📝 Retake course
  exam" pill appears on your subject card. Take it as often as
  you want; every attempt updates your "latest" score.

Your full attempt history is on the summative review page so you
can see growth over time.

## Help / Feedback

There's a Help button you can use anywhere on the platform.

- **In a lesson chat** → tap the `?` icon in the **chat header**
  (next to the speaker / audio icon). On phones, find it in the
  three-dot overflow menu under "Help / report an issue".
- **On any other page** → the floating Help / Feedback button
  bottom-right.

Both open the same modal with two tabs:

- **💬 Ask the AI** — a chatbot trained on the platform docs.
  Ask "how do I…", "where is…", "why did I get this wrong". It
  knows you're a student and won't suggest teacher-only steps.
- **📩 Send to support** — text + optional screenshot capture
  forwarded to platform admins.

## Email verification

The verification banner at the top of every page asks you to
verify your email so weekly reminders reach you. Click the
"Resend link" button if your verification email got lost. You
can dismiss the banner with the × button if it's distracting,
but you'll keep missing reminders until you verify.

## Common problems

### "The page looks frozen"
Refresh. If it persists, click Help / Feedback with the page URL.

### "I get logged out unexpectedly"
Sessions last 2 weeks. If you're being kicked out sooner, your
school may have stricter cookie settings — let us know.

### "The tutor said something wrong"
The platform now runs an LLM-based safety judge on every tutor
message. If it catches something inappropriate, the message is
rewritten before you see it. For tutoring mistakes (wrong math,
confusing explanation), tap the Help button → "Send to support"
and describe what happened. Your teacher will be notified.

### "Voice / audio doesn't play"
Some browsers block autoplay audio. Tap the speaker icon to start
it manually. On mobile, make sure your silent switch is off.

### "Images aren't loading"
Slow internet — give it a few seconds. The platform caches images
so a second visit is faster.

### "The Retake course exam button isn't showing"
It only appears once you have at least one completed attempt on
the published summative. Take the baseline first.
